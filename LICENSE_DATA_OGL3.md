# Data licence and attribution

The source National Student Survey data used in this repository were published by the Office for Students (OfS).

OfS states that content it owns, including data, is available for re-use under the Open Government Licence (OGL), unless otherwise stated.

The compact data extracts in `data/raw_public_extracts/` and derived/harmonised files in `data/processed/` remain subject to the terms applicable to the underlying OfS data.

Required attribution:

> Contains Office for Students National Student Survey data, re-used under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
